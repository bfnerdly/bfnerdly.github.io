## Direction contract
THESIS: The site is a behavior map. Brandon's work appears the way his data does: scattered points that settle into
four labeled regions (C1 Model the data, C2 Build the tool, C3 Design the study, C4 Test the cause). It refuses the
portfolio template (name hero beside a portrait, stats strip, icon-card grid, chip clouds, kickers) and the faculty CV.

OWN-WORLD: One colormap owns every color: magma, from #0D0716 plum-black through #3B0F70, #8C2981, #DE4968, #FE9F6D
to #FCFDBF. Dark density field with smooth magma heat, thin contour lines, ghost contours in empty space, a dust of
points. Reading surfaces are cool plot-paper white (dark theme: the field's black). Archivo (variable width) for all
text, region labels in expanded tracked caps like cartography; Martian Mono only for IDs, axes, DOIs, counts. Square
2px corners, hairline rules, no shadows, no cards-as-structure; figures carry "Fig." captions.

STORY: The visitor sees messy data become clear structure, reads "Brandon Fricker, PhD: I turn messy,
high-dimensional behavior data into clear answers, and build the tools that get there", sees each region backed by
real projects and papers, reads one experiment laid out like an A/B readout, and emails him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: full-bleed dark map field (about 88vh) with axis ticks ("dim 1", "dim 2"); the four regions
sit in the right 60% with linked labels; a legend panel at left holds a small portrait, name at display scale, role,
pitch, and primary "Email me" plus "Résumé"/LinkedIn; caption bottom right marks the map illustrative. Mobile: map
band on top with region labels, legend panel overlapping its lower edge with the name and actions inside 812px.
Signature interaction: points start as uniform noise and settle into the clusters once on load (static under reduced
motion); hovering or focusing a region or its key lights that region and every project and paper tagged with it
across the page (linked brushing).

FORM: The Behavior Map (behavior-space density maps / UMAP), rank 6 of 7 on my grounded list; seed key 2e984f4d.
Raises: map states from the rain-night city; linked brushing from the Miura fold; ghost contours from the
seven-segment display; one cluster-ID labeling system from the crew terminal; total colormap economy from the
one-bit desktop.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
